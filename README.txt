GTM Ledger - installable version

What is in this folder
  index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, 3 icons

Put it online once (needs a computer)
  1. Unzip this file so you have the gtm-ledger folder.
  2. Go to app.netlify.com/drop in a browser on your computer.
  3. Drag the whole gtm-ledger folder onto the page.
  4. Netlify gives you a link ending in .netlify.app. It may ask you to make a
     free account so the site is not deleted later. Do that.

Install it on your Android phone
  1. Open that link in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu, then "Add to Home Screen" or "Install app".
  3. Open GTM Ledger from your home screen. After the first load it also works offline.

Notes
  - Entries are saved on the phone only. Use Backup now and then. It downloads a
    file, and Restore loads it back, even on a new phone.
  - The version inside Claude uses the same backup format, so you can Backup there
    and Restore here to move your entries over.
  - Clearing Chrome's site data for this page erases the entries, so keep a backup.
